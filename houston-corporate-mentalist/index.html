About Shine, Houston's Corporate Mentalist

Houston's corporate calendar runs on energy-sector conferences, Fortune 500 headquarters events, and galas tied to one of the largest medical centers in the world. It's a city where the audience in the room is often making real decisions, not just attending a party. Shine spent several years as a technology executive before taking the stage, so he knows how to read that kind of room.

He's performed corporate shows across Houston — including for teams at Chevron, HPE, and Sysco — and tailors every performance to the caliber of the event, from an investor dinner to a company-wide holiday party, customized with your company name, product, or event theme.

Other companies that have booked Shine include Google, CDW, Climb, Adaptive Health, The Difference Card, and Pepsi.
