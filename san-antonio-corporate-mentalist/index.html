Why Companies Book Shine

About Shine, San Antonio's Corporate Mentalist

San Antonio's economy runs on hospitality — conventions, downtown hotel ballrooms, and a hosting standard that's hard to match anywhere else in Texas. Add a major military and government contractor presence and one of the country's largest medical centers, and San Antonio corporate audiences are used to events that are genuinely well-run, not just well-attended.

Shine has performed corporate shows across San Antonio — including for teams at Cognizant, USAA, and H-E-B — and builds every performance to meet that bar: interactive mentalism that's clean enough for a conservative government or healthcare crowd, and sharp enough to headline a downtown convention gala. Every show is customized with your company name, product, or event theme.

Other companies that have booked Shine include Google, CDW, Climb, Adaptive Health, The Difference Card, and Pepsi.
